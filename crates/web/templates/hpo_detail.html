Parameter Importance (fANOVA — per objective)

Main-effect variance ratios from Study::fanova() (optimizer crate, unaltered), computed separately for each NSGA-III objective — OK@1%, param count, and max error. Longer bar = that hyperparameter explains more variance of that objective across trials. The final (interactions) bar (grey, dashed) is the aggregate variance explained by pairs (and higher orders) of parameters acting together — fANOVA normalises so Σ main_effects + Σ interactions = 100%. A large interactions bar means the objective is driven by hyperparameter combinations, not by any single knob. Typical ranges in this project: param_count is low-interaction (~20%, dominated by hidden_size); ok_at_1pct and max_error are mid-to-high (~40–55%). Legacy studies — and the Spearman fallback above ~2k trials — have no interactions data and omit the bar.
